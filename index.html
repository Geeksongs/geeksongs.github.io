I graduated with a master’s degree from Columbia University. My research interests lie in robot learning, foundation models, and AI for science:

Robot Learning: I study world models and reinforcement learning to push toward the ChatGPT moment for robotics, with the goal of uncovering scaling laws for robot learning and enabling more general, and dexterous robot intelligence.

Foundation Models: Researching reasoning in vision-language models and building next-generation AI with the long-term goal of developing self-consciousness.

I conducted research under the guidance of Prof. Jesse Thomason at Georgia Tech, and Prof. Junfeng Yang and Prof. Shilong Liu at Columbia. I have also had the privilege of conducting research on robot learning with Research Scientists Siddharth Ancha at UC Berkeley and Ajay Mandlekar at NVIDIA.

Foundation Model

Reasoning and self-consciousness in vision-language and next-generation AI models.

World Model

Learning predictive world models to uncover scaling laws for robot learning.

Embodied AI

Enabling general, dexterous robot intelligence through embodied reasoning and control.
